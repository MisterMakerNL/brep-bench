Performance

What each kernel's runs cost over the cases: their time, CPU time and memory, and how well they use many cores. A time here is the kernel's own, building the solid, measuring it and writing its triangles: what starting its runner costs is left out, which is far more for a Python or .NET runner than a Rust one, and is shown on its own. None of it is graded, and all of it depends on the machine of the last run.
