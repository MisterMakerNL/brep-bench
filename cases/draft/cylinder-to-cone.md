A cylinder of radius 10 on the z axis, from z = 0 to z = 20, its side drafted 3° about its base:
the solid is pulled along +z, the neutral plane is z = 0, and the side leans in 3° from the
vertical as it rises.

So the side is a cone, and the solid a frustum: radius 10 at its base, z = 0, and
10 − 20·tan 3° ≈ 8.952 at its top, z = 20.
