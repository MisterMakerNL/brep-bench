A square plate from (−20, −20, 0) to (20, 20, 8) with a bore of radius 8 on the z axis right
through it, drafted as a moulded part is: every wall drafted 3° about the base, with one pull.
The solid is pulled along +z, the neutral plane is z = 0, and the four outer sides and the bore's
wall each turn 3° about their bottom edges so that the material narrows as it rises: the outer
sides lean in, towards the axis, and the bore's wall leans out, away from it. The base and the
top stay horizontal.

So the section at height z is the 40 × 40 square moved z·tan 3° in on every side, less a disc of
radius 8 + z·tan 3°. The bore is a cone, of radius 8 at the base and 8 + 8·tan 3° ≈ 8.419 at the
top; the top is a square about 39.161 across.
