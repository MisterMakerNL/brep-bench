A box from (0, 0, 0) to (30, 20, 10) with one side drafted: the face x = 30 turns 10° about its
bottom edge (the solid is pulled along +z, the neutral plane is z = 0) so that it leans in as it
rises, meeting the top at x = 30 − 10·tan 10° ≈ 28.237. No other face is drafted.

The faces beside it, y = 0 and y = 20, stay where they are and are trimmed by the leaning face
into trapezoids; the top shrinks to 28.237 × 20; the base and the face x = 0 are unchanged.
