A plate, the box from the origin to (40, 30, 8), drilled right through along z by a hole of
radius 5 whose axis is the vertical line through (20, 15).

Then edit it directly: replace the surface of the hole's wall, the cylinder, by the cone about the
same axis whose radius is 5 at z = 0 and 7 at z = 8, widening upward, and trim the faces around it
to meet the new surface. The bottom's hole stays a circle of radius 5; the top's is widened to a
circle of radius 7.

The result is the box from the origin to (40, 30, 8) with a tapered hole through it along the line
through (20, 15): the frustum of that cone from z = 0 to z = 8 removed.
