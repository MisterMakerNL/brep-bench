A plate, the box from the origin to (40, 30, 8), drilled right through along z by a hole of
radius 5 whose axis is the vertical line through (20, 15).

Then edit it directly: delete the hole's wall, and heal the gap it leaves by extending the faces
around it along their own surfaces. The top and the bottom each close over the hole's circle.

The result is the box from the origin to (40, 30, 8).
