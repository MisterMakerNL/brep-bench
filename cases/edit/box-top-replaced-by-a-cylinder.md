A box from the origin to (20, 10, 10).

Then edit it directly: replace the surface of its top face, the plane z = 10, by the cylinder of
radius 10√2 whose axis is the line x = 10, z = 0, parallel to the y axis, and trim and extend the
faces around it to meet the new surface. The cylinder passes through the top's two edges on
x = 0 and x = 20, where it meets the ends at z = 10 as before, and rises to z = 10√2 over x = 10.
The ends x = 0 and x = 20 stay as they were; the sides y = 0 and y = 10 are extended upward to the
cylinder, each becoming the rectangle's base and sides capped by a circular arc.

The result is a barrel-vaulted block: the region over the rectangle from (0, 0) to (20, 10) in the
plane z = 0, from z = 0 up to the cylinder.
