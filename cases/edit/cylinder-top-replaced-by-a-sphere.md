A cylinder of radius 6 about the z axis, from z = 0 to z = 10.

Then edit it directly: replace the surface of its top face, the plane z = 10, by the sphere of
radius 10 centered at (0, 0, 2), and trim the faces around it to meet the new surface. The sphere
meets the cylinder's side in the circle of radius 6 at z = 10, where the flat top's rim was, so the
side is unchanged; of the sphere, the top keeps only the cap above z = 10, a dome rising to its
pole at (0, 0, 12). (The sphere meets the cylinder again at z = −6, below the bottom; that has no
part in the result.)

The result is the cylinder of radius 6 from z = 0 to z = 10 with the dome, a spherical cap of
height 2 cut from a ball of radius 10, set on its top.
