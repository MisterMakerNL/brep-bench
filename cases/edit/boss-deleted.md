A plate, the box from the origin to (40, 30, 5), with a round boss standing on its top: the
cylinder of radius 6 about the vertical line through (20, 15), from z = 5 to z = 15, joined to the
plate.

Then edit it directly: delete the boss's side and its top, and heal the gap they leave by
extending the faces around them along their own surfaces. The plate's top closes over the circle
where the boss stood.

The result is the box from the origin to (40, 30, 5).
