A ball of radius 5 centered on the origin, with a hole of radius 3 bored right through it along
the z axis: `boolean/napkin-ring` again. The hole's wall meets the sphere in two circles of
radius 3, at z = 4 and z = −4, the rims of the ring.

This case is about a rim passing close by a pole without running through it, as it does in
`singular/napkin-ring-poles-on-its-rims`. If the ball you make has poles, put them on the line
through the origin along (3.1, 0, 4): each is then on the ring's sphere, a little under a degree
(0.079 along the sphere) outside a rim, the upper rim passing between the upper pole and the z
axis. If you make the hole with a cylinder, run it past both ends of the ball.
