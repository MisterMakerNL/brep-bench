A ball of radius 5 centred on the origin, with a hole of radius 3 bored right through it along
the z axis: `boolean/napkin-ring` again. The hole's wall meets the sphere in two circles of
radius 3, at z = 4 and z = −4, the rims of the ring.

This case is about those rims running through the ball's poles. If the ball you make has poles,
put them on the line through the origin along (3, 0, 4): at (3, 0, 4), on the upper rim, and at
(−3, 0, −4), on the lower one. If you make the hole with a cylinder, run it past both ends of the
ball.
