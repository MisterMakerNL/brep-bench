A cylinder of radius 5 on the z axis, from z = −1 to z = 10, cut by two planes: keep the part above
the plane z = 0 and below the plane z = x tan 30°, which meets the first along the y axis and rises
toward +x.

What is left is a hoof (an ungula): its flat base is the half disk of radius 5 where x ≥ 0, its
slanting top is half an ellipse, and its curved side, a part of the cylinder, runs from height 0
at the points (0, ±5, 0) to 5 tan 30° at (5, 0, 5 tan 30°). Base, top and side all meet at
(0, ±5, 0), where the side narrows to nothing.
