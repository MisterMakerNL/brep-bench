A solid cone on the z axis: its base the disc of radius 5 in the xy plane centred on the origin,
its apex at (0, 0, 10). Cut it by the plane 4x + z = 10, which passes through the apex and meets
the base plane in the line x = 2.5, and cut away the side where 4x + z > 10, away from the axis.

What is left is a cone over the base less its segment beyond x = 2.5 (the chord from
(2.5, −2.5√3, 0) to (2.5, 2.5√3, 0) cuts off a third of the rim): two thirds of the side, the
base less the segment, and the triangle where it was cut, from that chord up to the apex. The
plane meets the cone's side only in the two rulings through the chord's ends, which meet at the
apex.
