A ball of radius 5 centered on the origin, cut by the plane x + z = 5, which leans at 45° and
passes through (0, 0, 5) and (5, 0, 0). Cut away the cap on the side away from the center, where
x + z > 5, and keep the rest. The plane is 5/√2 from the center, so the cap is 5(1 − 1/√2)
high, and the cut face is a disk of radius 5/√2 centered on (2.5, 0, 2.5).

This case is about a cut that runs through a sphere's pole without following a meridian. If the
ball you make has poles, put them on the z axis: the rim of the cut then passes through the pole
at (0, 0, 5), where it crosses every meridian.
