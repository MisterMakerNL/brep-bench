A block, the box from the origin to (30, 20, 10).

Imprint on its top, the face z = 10, the circle of radius 5 centered at (15, 10, 10): the circle
becomes an edge of the solid, and splits the top into two faces, the disk inside it and the rest of
the top around it, without changing the solid. Then push the disk face straight down by 3, along
−z, its rim sweeping a new wall as it goes, so that it becomes the floor of a pocket. Do not cut the
pocket with a cylinder: the case is about the imprinted face, and moving it.

The result is the box from the origin to (30, 20, 10) less the cylinder of radius 5 about the
vertical line through (15, 10) from z = 7 to z = 10: a round pocket 3 deep.
