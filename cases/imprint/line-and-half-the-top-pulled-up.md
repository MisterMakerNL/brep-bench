A block, the box from the origin to (30, 20, 10).

Imprint on its top, the face z = 10, the line x = 12 from edge to edge, from (12, 0, 10) to
(12, 20, 10): it becomes an edge of the solid, and splits the top into two faces, the rectangle
x ≤ 12 and the rectangle x ≥ 12, without changing the solid. Then pull the face x ≤ 12 straight up
by 4, along +z, to the plane z = 14. The faces beside it, the end x = 0 and the sides y = 0 and
y = 20, run on up to meet it, and where it parts from the rest of the top, along the imprinted
edge, a new wall on the plane x = 12 rises from z = 10 to z = 14.

The result is the box from the origin to (30, 20, 10) joined with the box from (0, 0, 10) to
(12, 20, 14): a step 4 high.
