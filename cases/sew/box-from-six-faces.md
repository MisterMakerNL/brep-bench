Six separate faces, each a rectangle on its own plane and none sharing an edge or a vertex with
another: the six faces of the box from the origin to (20, 10, 5), on the planes x = 0, x = 20,
y = 0, y = 10, z = 0 and z = 5. Make each face on its own, from its own four corners, without
regard to which way it faces.

Sew the six into one closed shell, so that each pair of faces meeting at an edge of the box shares
that edge, and make the solid the shell bounds, faced outward. Do not build the box from a box
primitive or an extrusion: the case is about joining faces that were made apart.

The solid is the box from the origin to (20, 10, 5).
