Four separate faces that share no edge or vertex, each a quarter of the surface of the torus about
the z axis whose tube has radius 3 and whose tube's center runs on the circle of radius 10 in the
plane z = 0. Each quarter is the part of that surface between two half planes through the z axis:
the first between the half planes at 0° and 90° from the +x axis, turning toward +y, and the
others between 90° and 180°, 180° and 270°, and 270° and 360°. Each is a face made on its own, its
two ends circles of radius 3, and each is closed round its tube on its own seam.

Sew the four into one closed shell, so that each quarter's end circle and its neighbor's become
one edge, and make the solid the shell bounds, faced outward. Do not use a torus primitive or a
revolution of a disk: the case is about joining faces that were made apart, each wrapped round on
itself.

The solid is the ring torus of major radius 10 and minor radius 3 about the z axis.
